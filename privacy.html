fleet-readonly - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This application ("fleet-readonly") is operated by a private individual for personal, non-commercial use only.

What it accesses

It accesses only the account holder's own bank account information (balances and transactions) in read-only mode, through Enable Banking's licensed account-information service. It cannot initiate payments and cannot access anyone else's accounts.

How data is used

Retrieved account data is used solely to give the account holder a private overview of their own finances. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Storage and security

Data is stored securely on the operator's own private server and used only for the purpose above. Access credentials are held encrypted and are never shared.
